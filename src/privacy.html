Privacy

How this website handles your information

Last updated 16 November 2026

What we collect

This website does not store anything you type. The tour form builds a WhatsApp message on your own device and opens WhatsApp. We only receive your details if you press send in WhatsApp.

When you message us on WhatsApp, we keep your name, number and what you asked about so we can arrange your visit and reply to you.

Services from other companies

  • Google Maps shows our location, but only after you press “Show the map”. Google may then set cookies under the Google privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp carries any message you send us, under the WhatsApp privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies and doesn’t track you across other sites. See the Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics shows us how many people visit, which pages they read and how they found us. It sets cookies on your device and records a shortened version of your IP address. It never receives what you type into the tour form. See the Google privacy policy, or block it with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Fonts and images are served from this website, not from third parties.

What we don’t do

We don’t use advertising trackers on this site, and we don’t sell or share your details. The only cookies are the Google Analytics ones described above, plus Google Maps if you open the map.

Your choices
